♻ Waste Awareness

Wet Waste: Food scraps and biodegradable waste should be separated for composting.
Dry Waste: Paper, cardboard, plastic and metal should be separated from wet waste.
E-Waste: Old electronic items should be given to proper recycling channels.
3R Rule: Reduce, Reuse and Recycle.
Clean Environment: Always use dustbins and avoid throwing garbage on roads or public places.
